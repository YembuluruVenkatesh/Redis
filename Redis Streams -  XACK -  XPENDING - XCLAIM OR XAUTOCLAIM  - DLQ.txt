XPENDING (Pending Entries List): 

Why is XPENDING useful?

Suppose your application processes payments.

Receive Payment Event
        │
        ▼
Debit Customer
        │
        ▼
💥 Server Crash

Without Redis Streams:

❌ Payment event is lost.

With Redis Streams:

Pending Entries List

Payment Event
Status = Pending

When the service restarts, it can continue processing.
